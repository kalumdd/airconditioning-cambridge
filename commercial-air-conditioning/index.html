Commercial air conditioning in Cambridge

Cambridge has an unusual mix of commercial buildings: offices around the station and the CB1 area, research and tech companies on the Science Park, St John's Innovation Park and Peterhouse Technology Park, clinics and labs near the Biomedical Campus, and shops and restaurants in the historic centre. Each needs a different approach to cooling, access and working hours.

For organisations with sites across the region, our parent company Anglian Aircon runs maintenance contracts with one point of contact and one set of records.

What we install for businesses

  • Four-way cassettes in suspended ceilings for open offices, shops and cafés.
  • Concealed ducted units where only discreet grilles should show.
  • High-wall and floor units for meeting rooms, consulting rooms and small offices.
  • VRF/VRV for multi-zone buildings, with heat recovery where zones need heating and cooling at once.
  • Close control and comms room cooling with duty/standby units and alarms.

Fitting around the working day

Every project gets a written proposal covering equipment, layouts, programme and price. Work is planned around opening hours, lab access rules and occupied floors, with evening or weekend working where needed and daily updates.

Keeping it compliant

  • Planned preventative maintenance with a schedule and reports
  • F-Gas leak checks at the right frequency and records kept up to date
  • Advice on TM44 energy inspections where total cooling exceeds 12 kW
  • Priority call-outs for contract customers

Clients

Owners and managers of offices, labs, clinics and shops, landlords and agents, schools and colleges, and main contractors who need a dependable air conditioning package.
