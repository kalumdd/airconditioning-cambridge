Cherry Hinton mixes 1930s semis and post-war family homes with some of Cambridge's best-known technology employers, including Arm at Peterhouse Technology Park. A lot of people here work from home part of the week, and a lot of offices here are full of equipment.

Homes, home offices and tech offices

1930s and post-war houses usually have cavity walls and side access, which makes for straightforward installations. The rooms we cool most are loft conversions and back bedrooms used as home offices, where screens and computers add heat all day.

For offices and labs, equipment loads matter more than floor area. We size systems for the real heat in the room and cool comms rooms with units designed to run all year.
