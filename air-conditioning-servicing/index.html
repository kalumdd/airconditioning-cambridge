Servicing in Cambridge

Our Cambridge servicing work ranges from single bedroom units to systems in offices, clinics and labs that need records for audits and insurers. Landlords and letting agents use a plan so every unit across their properties is serviced once a year with the paperwork in one place.

For labs and equipment rooms we agree visit times that work around experiments and access rules, and record every check for your compliance file.

What happens on a service visit

  • Filters removed, washed and checked for damage
  • Indoor coil and fan barrel cleaned and treated with an anti-bacterial product
  • Drain tray and pipe flushed, pump tested if fitted
  • Outdoor coil cleaned, fan and fixings checked
  • Operating pressures and temperatures logged, joints leak-tested
  • Wiring, isolators and controls inspected
  • Heating and cooling performance tested
  • Written report, with photos and any recommendations

How often?

Homes: once a year, ideally in spring. Busy offices, labs, clinics, salons, gyms and server rooms: every six months, because longer running hours and more dust load up filters and coils faster. Extended manufacturer warranties often depend on a yearly service.

When to book sooner

  • The room takes noticeably longer to reach temperature
  • A damp or musty smell at start-up
  • Drips or water marks under the indoor unit
  • Rattling, buzzing or a louder outdoor unit
  • Frost on the pipework in summer
  • Flashing lights or an error code on the display

Service plans

A plan books your visits in advance, sends a reminder and moves you up the queue if a fault appears. It suits landlords, letting agents and any business with several units.

F-Gas obligations

Systems holding 5 tonnes CO₂ equivalent of refrigerant or more must be leak-checked by certified engineers at set intervals, with records kept by the operator. We do the checks and keep the records for you.

FAQs

Servicing: common questions

How long is a service visit?

Around an hour for a single split, longer for multi-splits and commercial systems.

Will you service a system someone else fitted?

Yes, most makes. Tell us the make and model when booking.

What is the typical price?

UK prices for a home system usually fall between £100 and £200 a year. You get a fixed price when you book, and plans cost less per visit.
