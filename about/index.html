Who we are

Straight answers, careful work.

Air Conditioning Cambridge is part of Anglian Aircon, an East Anglian air conditioning and heat pump company with local teams across Suffolk, Norfolk, Essex and Cambridgeshire.

We install, service and repair air conditioning and air-to-air heat pumps for homeowners, landlords and businesses in Cambridge and the surrounding villages. Most people come to us wanting a straight answer: what size of system, where it should go, what it will cost to buy and run, and whether it is worth it.

Being part of a regional company means we can handle larger commercial projects and maintenance contracts across several sites, while you deal with one team that knows the area.

How we work

  • Free, written, itemised quotes with the make and model named
  • Systems sized for the room, not the biggest unit we can sell
  • Pipework kept short, straight and neatly trunked
  • Pressure tested, vacuumed and commissioned properly
  • A clear handover and a reminder when a service is due
  • Honest repair-or-replace advice

Equipment

We install and service equipment from established manufacturers such as Daikin, Mitsubishi Electric and Toshiba, and recommend the model that suits the room, noise expectations, controls and budget.
